Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how "Water Note" (the "App") handles your personal information.

Information We Collect

The App stores the following information only on your device:

  • Water intake records (amount, date and time)
  • App settings (daily goal, reminder settings)

Where Information Is Stored

All data is stored exclusively on your device. No data is transmitted to any external servers.

How We Use Information

The stored information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Recording and displaying water intake
  • Calculating and displaying statistics
  • Sending reminder notifications

Third-Party Sharing

The App does not share any collected information with third parties.

Notifications

The App uses notification permissions for the reminder feature. You can enable or disable notifications at any time from the settings screen within the App.

Data Deletion

All stored data will be deleted when you uninstall the App.

Contact Us

Email: appstake35@gmail.com

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated without prior notice. Any changes will be posted on this page.
